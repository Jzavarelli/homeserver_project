# Personal Zav Homelab
A walkthrough of the layout, design, creation, and evoluation of my Homelabbing process for others to view and follow as my learning continues. The following breaks down the different deployments, modules, setups, connections, APIs, and other items I have built to create my Homelab.

I started a Homelab, on a budget, because I wanted to expand my skillset and dive into a world of technology I had not experienced before. I hope to also cover something I keep running into while working on a Homelab; **support, support, and support**. I feel the world of creating a Server lands between gatekeeping and assumed knowledge which should see a bridge from the most advanced to the most beginning of Users. 

## System Setup
I wanted to have a starting point for my Homelab, a starting point with a seemingly unconventional approach to building a solution. I decided to pick a piece of hardware and software which are not normally combined and have some strong criticism. I expect people to judge how my solution is not efficient and creates unecessary problems, but I believe my choice has given me insight to possibility and allows me to speak from new perspectives with Homelabbing.
